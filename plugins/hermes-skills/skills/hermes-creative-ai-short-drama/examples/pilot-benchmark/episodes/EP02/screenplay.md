# EP02 Screenplay — The Drawer

Target: 60 seconds. Location: `LOC_REPAIR_SHOP`, continuous from EP01.

1. Lin holds `PROP_KEY` beside the still-closed drawer. LIN: “我只问一次，里面是什么？”
2. Zhao reaches toward the lock, then stops his own hand. ZHAO: “你打开它，就会有人知道你回来了。”
3. Lin moves the key to the lock without changing hands.
4. Zhao steps aside, clearing her path. ZHAO: “这把钥匙，是你妈妈配的。”
5. Lin turns the key once; the drawer opens and reveals `PROP_CASSETTE`.
6. Lin lifts the cassette while retaining the key. A clean-label plate receives the post overlay: “给小林，零点后播放.” Zhao looks toward the front door.

Scene end: drawer open; key and cassette with Lin; key origin revealed; Zhao's role unresolved.
