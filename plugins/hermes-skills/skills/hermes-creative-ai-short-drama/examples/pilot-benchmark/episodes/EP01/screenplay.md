# EP01 Screenplay — The Key

Target: 60 seconds. Location: `LOC_REPAIR_SHOP`, interior, night.

1. Lin places a delivery parcel on the counter; `PROP_KEY` slips beside it. Zhao freezes. LIN: “你见过这把钥匙？”
2. Zhao covers his reaction by turning over the parcel label. ZHAO: “没见过。”
3. Lin raises the key between them. LIN: “那你为什么不敢看我？”
4. Zhao's eyes shift once toward the hidden drawer behind him.
5. Lin follows his eyeline and sees the drawer handle.
6. A blue thread tied to the handle moves in the door draft, matching the thread on her key. LIN: “这是我妈留下的。” Cut before Zhao answers.

Scene end: the drawer stays closed; Lin retains the key; both know the other recognized the connection.
