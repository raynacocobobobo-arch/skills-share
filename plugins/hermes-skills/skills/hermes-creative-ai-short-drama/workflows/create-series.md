# Create Series

Stage order: `Greenlight -> Series Bible -> 3–5 episode pilot outline -> persist canonical state`

## Inputs

- original concept or adaptation brief;
- target audience, format, approximate episode length, and production constraints when known;
- existing approved Series Bible when revising rather than starting.

Read [story architecture](../references/story-architecture.md) and use [series-bible.md](../templates/series-bible.md).

## Procedure

1. **Greenlight:** express the premise, audience promise, central cost, escalation runway, production repeatability, and cheapest pilot test. Choose `GO_PILOT`, `REVISE`, or `STOP`.
2. **Series Bible:** on `GO_PILOT`, assign stable IDs and fill premise, characters, world rules, recurring locations, Entity Registry, Reveal Ladder, Antagonist Ladder, and Payoff Debt Ledger.
3. **Pilot outline:** plan 3–5 episodes. Give each one a distinct opening hook, single dominant turn, payoff/progress, cliffhanger, intended State Delta, and pilot hypothesis.
4. **Persist canonical state:** version the approved Series Bible and record `canonical through: EP00`. Keep unknown production-critical facts as `TBD`.

Stop expansion and return `REVISE` or `STOP` when the concept lacks escalation runway, repeatable production anchors, or meaningful differentiation across the pilot. Do not outline the full season by default.

## Outputs

- Greenlight decision with one reason and next action;
- versioned Series Bible;
- 3–5 episode pilot outline;
- canonical initial state ready for `create-episode.md`.
