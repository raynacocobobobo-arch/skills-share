# Photography Reality Matching

## Purpose

Ensure digital humans appear captured by the original camera rather than composited.

## Matching Dimensions

### Camera

Analyze:

- focal length
- camera height
- perspective distortion
- framing

### Lighting

Match:

- key light direction
- color temperature
- contrast
- shadow direction

### Environment

Match:

- depth of field
- image sharpness
- noise pattern
- color response

## Common Failure Modes

Avoid:

- floating characters
- incorrect scale
- inconsistent exposure
- studio lighting in outdoor scenes
- artificial skin texture

The target is real photography consistency.
