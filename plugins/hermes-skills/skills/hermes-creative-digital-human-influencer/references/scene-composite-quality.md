# Scene Composite Quality

## Goal

The digital human must appear captured in the original environment.

## Checks

### Geometry

- correct scale
- correct perspective
- realistic placement

### Lighting

- matching light direction
- matching exposure
- matching color temperature

### Contact

- feet contact
- shadows
- environmental interaction

### Photography

- depth of field consistency
- image texture consistency
- realistic camera characteristics

Reject images where the person appears pasted onto the scene.
