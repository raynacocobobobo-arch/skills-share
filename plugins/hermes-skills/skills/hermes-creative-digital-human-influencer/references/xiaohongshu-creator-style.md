# Xiaohongshu Creator Style

## Positioning

The target is a realistic lifestyle creator, not an AI model showcase.

## Preferred Characteristics

- natural photography
- authentic lifestyle scenes
- believable imperfections
- environment storytelling

## Avoid

- excessive beauty retouching
- synthetic skin
- studio model appearance
- unrealistic proportions

## Typical Scenes

- travel
- hotels
- cafes
- city lifestyle
- product experience
