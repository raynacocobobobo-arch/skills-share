# Outfit Transfer Workflow

## Goal

Replace clothing while preserving digital human identity.

## Input

- existing digital human asset
- clothing reference image
- style requirement

## Preserve

- face identity
- body proportions
- age appearance
- personal style

## Change

- clothing
- shoes
- accessories
- styling

## Validation

Check that clothing follows:

- body perspective
- lighting direction
- material behavior

The output should look like the person actually wore the outfit during the photo shoot.
